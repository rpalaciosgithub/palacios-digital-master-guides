This app intentionally uses multiple sources because no single source is perfect. Google Maps is the main door-to-door route and alternate-route tool. Waze opens separately as a desktop Live Map for another look at current traffic reports. Caltrans/CHP provide official incidents and closures.

Always follow posted signs and current road closures. Do not interact with the app while driving.
