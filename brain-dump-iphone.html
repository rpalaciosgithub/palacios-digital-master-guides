How this iPhone version works

Notes stay in this app on this device. Export a category to create an RTF copy, then choose Save to Files or your email app.

Exports contain every saved note in that category, newest first. They do not automatically update a previous export.

Keep a backup: clearing website data can remove these notes. Your Windows app and this app do not sync automatically.

For your Home Screen: upload this HTML to GitHub Pages, open its link in Safari, then use Share → Add to Home Screen. Use the same link each time. An HTML attachment preview is not the running app.

1. Capture your thoughts

How to use tags

You can type or click a tag, then write your note. Add more tags and notes as needed, then tap Organize & save. Each note goes to its own category.

@todo    call dentist

Or:

@todo
call dentist

Several notes:

@todo
call dentist

@quote Our hearts are restless until they rest in you

2. Your saved notes

Open a category to read or export it. RTF exports use 20-point text: red headings and bold blue notes.

3. Backup & transfer

Backup includes all categories, notes, and your draft. Restore merges saved notes without duplicating them.

For Windows: open the tagged RTF, copy its text into your desktop Brain Dump, review, and organize. Exported notes remain here; transferring the same notes twice can create duplicates on Windows.
