🚗 Car Loans & Interest

Learn what a car really costs before a salesperson sells you a “great monthly payment.”

1. The 5 numbers that matter

Do not focus only on the monthly payment. You need the whole deal.

What the car costs before financing.
Money paid up front.
The annual percentage rate — the price of borrowing money.
How long you will make payments.
Dealer says: “Only $299 a month!”
You ask: “For how many months? At what APR? How much am I financing? What is the total amount I will pay?”

4. Dealer Red Flags

🚩
“What monthly payment do you want?”A low payment can be created by stretching the loan for years.
🚩
“We can get you approved today!”Approval is not the same as a good loan.
🚩
“No credit? No problem!”Ask for the APR and total cost before celebrating.
🚩
72- or 84-month loansThey can make the payment look smaller while keeping you in debt much longer.
🚩
Dealer add-onsWarranties, protection packages and extras can increase the amount financed.
Before signing, ask for the out-the-door price, APR, term, amount financed, monthly payment, and total of payments.
